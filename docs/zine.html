CHOMPI firmwaresone at a timemade by the community

Feed theCHOMPI

Send a firmware over USB, keep it in one of the launcher's fifteen slots, and start it right away. Send to the same slot again and the new version replaces the old one.

This builds on the multi-firmware launcher by @sfaber02, which does the hard part of running several firmwares from one card. Firmware upload over USB is part of it since launcher v1.1.

4 steps · one firmware at a time · Chrome or Edge

Use at your own riskThis is an unofficial community project, not made or supported by CHOMPI Club, and it comes without any warranty. Sending a firmware writes to your SD card and replaces whatever is in the slot you pick, so back up your card first. If something goes wrong, switch CHOMPI off and on.

Several at once?The setup page puts firmwares on all fifteen keys in one go.

First time? Set up the card
  1. Back up your SD card: copy everything on it to your computer.
  2. Download chompi-multi-firmware-card.zip from the latest launcher release and unzip it.
  3. Format a microSD card as FAT32 (32 GB or smaller).
  4. Copy everything inside chompi-multi-firmware-card/ to the top level of the card.
  5. Put the card in CHOMPI and switch it on. The first start shows a rainbow while the launcher is installed, then the picker: key 1 TAPE, key 2 TEMPO, key 3 WAVE, key 15 USB storage.
  6. Plug CHOMPI into this computer and continue below.
