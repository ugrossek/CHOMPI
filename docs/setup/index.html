Set up your CHOMPI

Test page

Choose which firmware goes on which key, then write them all to your CHOMPI in one go. No card reader, no files to copy.

Built on the multi-firmware launcher by @sfaber02. The firmwares are made by people in the CHOMPI community; each card below links to its release.

Demo. No CHOMPI here: this page is talking to a pretend one in your browser, so nothing is written anywhere. Reload to start over.

Use at your own risk. This is an unofficial community project, not made or supported by CHOMPI Club, and it comes without any warranty. It writes to your SD card and replaces what is on the keys you change, so back up your card first. If something goes wrong, switch CHOMPI off and on.

Test page. It needs a test version of the launcher (see step 1). The list of firmwares hasn't been checked with their authors yet.

  1. 1Connectnot connected

    Switch CHOMPI on so its keys are breathing white, plug it into this computer, then connect. Your browser will ask to allow MIDI; say yes. After that, this page connects by itself.

    This CHOMPI's launcher can only take one firmware at a time. This page needs the test launcher:

    1. Download CHOMPI.bin (test launcher).
    2. Switch CHOMPI off, take out the SD card and put it in your computer.
    3. Replace the CHOMPI.bin at the top level of the card with the downloaded one. Leave the FIRMWARE folder alone.
    4. Put the card back and switch CHOMPI on. A rainbow shows while it installs the new launcher.

  2. 2Choose firmwares

    Each white key on CHOMPI can hold one firmware. Tap a key, then choose what goes on it. Or tap Put on key on a firmware below.
