SING

Hold CHOMPI = menu

Press keys and sing, talk or make any sound: CHOMPI plays the keys' notes with your voice. Use headphones. Key 8 is middle C.

Robot

A vocoder, the Kraftwerk kind: every key plays a synth note that says what you say. Your pitch doesn't matter.

Try: talk, whisper, clap, a squeaky door. Size fully left = monster, right = mouse.

Human

Your real voice, moved onto the keys' notes, whatever you sing.

Try: sing near the keys' range. The key of the note you sing leaves your voice unchanged.

BUTTONS & SWITCH

Playnext character: Robot · Human
Loopfreeze the sound you're making (white); again: live
Togglelatch: notes keep sounding; the chord follows your voice (key 8 = your note). Press a key again to drop it
CHOMPIhold for the menu: menu lines above

LIGHTS

  • held keys red · middle C dim white
  • the note you sing: white in tune, red when off
  • white keys show a knob's position for a moment
  • chompi key red while latched, else your input level
  • wheel LEDs while frozen: red how far back, white now

OPTIONS.JSON (IN /SING)

  • Latch Follows Voice true: latched chords follow you
  • Voice Gate false: every noise plays along; true: only a voice
  • Show CPU false: true shows the load on the keys in the menu
  • Monitor Position 1 headphones, 2 all, 3 off

TRY

  • Hold C-E-G, say "hello", then turn size fully left
  • Sing "aaa", press loop, stop singing, play chords
  • Say a word, then turn the wheel back slowly
  • Latch a chord, sing a melody over it
  • An external mic avoids key clicks
